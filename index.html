What we run

Six services, one crew.

Every service below is delivered by the same technical team, which means one rig, one truck and one point of contact, with no coordinating between separate vendors on event day.

Video walls

LED screen rental

Fine pixel-pitch LED walls sized to your stage, from a backdrop panel for a wedding stage to a full concert-scale wall, with rigging and content playback handled on site.

Dance floors

LED dance floor

Modular illuminated flooring that syncs to music and lighting cues, the centrepiece for a sangeet, reception or club-style after-party.

Broadcast

Live streaming

Multi-camera live streams to YouTube, Instagram and private links, so family and guests who can't travel still watch in real time.

Rentals

TV rental service

Short and long-term television rentals for stages, registration desks, exhibitions and hospitality suites, delivered and mounted on schedule.

Stills

Photography

Candid and posed coverage across weddings, corporate events and brand shoots, delivered edited and ready to share.

Motion

Videography

Cinematic highlight films and full-length event edits, shot on the same rig that's already running your screens and stream.

How an event runs

From first call to strike, five stages.

01

Consult

We walk through your venue, guest count and moments that matter, then recommend the right mix of screens, floor and crew.

02

Design

Screen sizing, camera positions and streaming setup are mapped to your actual floor plan and run-of-show.

03

Install

Rigging, cabling and TVs go up ahead of schedule, with a full technical run-through before guests arrive.

04

Run live

Our crew stays on site through the event, operating screens, stream and cameras in sync with your program.

05

Deliver

Edited photos and film are handed over after the event, and every rented panel is struck the same night.
